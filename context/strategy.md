# @matteofights — Strategy

_Read for growth direction, cadence, and CTA decisions._

## North star
**Grow the fighter brand** — reach, watch-through, follower growth on @matteofights. Everything
else ladders off audience. When a call is ambiguous, pick the option that grows the account.

## The three jobs (in priority order)
1. **Grow** — build a real, engaged fighter audience around Matteo.
2. **Coaching lead-gen** — convert high-intent viewers to DMs ("DM me 'FIGHT'") for training-plan
   help; the seed of a paid coaching motion.
3. **Stance funnel** — Matteo's app gets *mentioned, not sold*. @matteofights is the top of that
   funnel. Dogfooding content ("here's my week in Stance") bridges to the product naturally.

## What drives growth here
- **Reels, not carousels.** Carousels flop for this account; reels are the entire game.
- **The listicle-rant format** (see [viral-format.md](viral-format.md)) is proven — replicate and
  vary it. Curiosity-gap hooks: `[NUMBER] WAYS TO [OUTCOME] WITHOUT [REQUIREMENT]`.
- **The origin story** (injury → build) is the pinned anchor that converts new visitors.
- **`READ DESCRIPTION ⬇️`** overlay drives caption expansions = engagement signal = more reach.

## Cadence
Consistency over volume. A sustainable reel rhythm beats sporadic bursts. Batch-make when possible
(the stat-reveal and listicle formats are batchable). Exact cadence: see [content.md](content.md).

## Measuring it (Phase 1)
Track per-reel **reach, watch-through (avg watch time), saves, and shares**, plus follower delta,
via Windsor. The winning reels tell us the next hook. Let the data choose topics — don't guess.

## Constraints
$0 budget mindset. Organic only. Recovery/surgery timeline is a real operational variable — content
should be producible even when Matteo can't train at full capacity (that limitation is itself the
content).
