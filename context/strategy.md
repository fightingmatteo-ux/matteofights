# @matteofights — Strategy

_Read for growth direction, cadence, and CTA decisions._

## North star
**Grow the fighter brand** — reach, watch-through, follower growth on @matteofights. Everything
else ladders off audience. When a call is ambiguous, pick the option that grows the account.

## The jobs (in priority order)
1. **Grow** — build a real, engaged audience. This is the north star and where the data is.
2. **Lead-gen → paid (soon).** Sequence Matteo confirmed: **audience-first now → drop a free lead
   magnet next → paid offer soon.** The DM is the capture point. **Standardized trigger: `HELP`**
   (one word everywhere — don't vary it per reel; keeps the funnel clean and trackable). The exact
   paid offer is intentionally undefined for now (Matteo will decide later, with "Jarvis").
3. **Stance — keep it OUT of @matteofights content for now.** Zero presence in the bio or 40 posts.
   Open possibility (Matteo's words): Stance early-access *might* later become the free lead magnet
   itself — TBD, his call. Until he says so: **no Stance mentions in content.**

## What drives growth here (data-confirmed 2026-06-05)
- **Reels, not carousels.** Carousels flop (<2k views); reels are the entire game.
- **Two listicle archetypes** (see [viral-format.md](viral-format.md)) — alternate them:
  - **Tactical** `[NUMBER] WAYS TO [OUTCOME] WITHOUT [REQUIREMENT]` → drives **saves** (the algo
    signal that went big: 160k reel had 1,937 saves).
  - **Identity** `[NUMBER] SIGNS YOU [ARE A REAL FIGHTER]` + "Repost this" → drives **shares**
    (the 320k reel that caused the current surge).
- **Watch-time gate ~10s+.** Front-load the hook; each numbered point resets attention.
- **Ride the surge.** As of 2026-06-05 the account is mid-spike — post more of both archetypes now.
- **Pin an origin-story reel** (injury → rebuild) to convert the flood of new visitors.

## Cadence
Consistency over volume. A sustainable reel rhythm beats sporadic bursts. Batch-make when possible
(the stat-reveal and listicle formats are batchable). Exact cadence: see [content.md](content.md).

## Measuring it (Phase 1)
Track per-reel **reach, watch-through (avg watch time), saves, and shares**, plus follower delta,
via Windsor. The winning reels tell us the next hook. Let the data choose topics — don't guess.

## Constraints
$0 budget mindset. Organic only. Recovery/surgery timeline is a real operational variable — content
should be producible even when Matteo can't train at full capacity (that limitation is itself the
content).
