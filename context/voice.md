# @matteofights — Voice

_Read before writing any caption, hook, or script._

## How it sounds
Raw and unfiltered. Short, punchy lines. Direct address — talk to the viewer, not about yourself.
Confident without hype. The tone of a good, experienced training partner who respects your
intelligence and doesn't waste your time.

Good: "You don't need to live in the gym to become dangerous."
Bad: "Unlock your potential and transform your training journey."

## Rules
- **Lead with a blunt or counterintuitive statement.** No warm-up.
- **Prefer second person over "I"** in hooks and openers — direct address pulls harder.
- **Short sentences. Active voice.** Say what you mean.
- **One idea per line.** Captions are scannable, not paragraphs.
- **End with one CTA.** Engagement CTA ("Drop a 🥊 if…") and/or conversion CTA ("DM me 'FIGHT'…").
- **2–4 hashtags, niche-specific.** Combat sports only.

## Language to use (mirror the audience)
"put in the work" / "stay dangerous" / "the work" / "roadwork" / "rounds" / "camp" / "stalking" /
"the pocket" / "feints" / "conditioning" / "discipline" / "show up" / "no excuses" (sparingly).

## Language to avoid
"wellness journey" / "holistic" / "self-care" / "mindfulness" / "unlock your potential" / "your
best self" / "transform your life" — anything that sounds like a Calm ad or a diet app. Also avoid
empty hype and guru-speak.

## Hashtags (rotate, keep to 2–4)
`#muaythai #boxing #fighter #martialarts #discipline #boxingtraining #sparring #boxinglife`

## Format note
Reels are the medium. Captions support the reel and trigger the "see more" expansion (which is why
reels carry a `READ DESCRIPTION ⬇️` overlay near the end — see [viral-format.md](viral-format.md)).
