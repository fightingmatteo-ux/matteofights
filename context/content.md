# @matteofights — Content Pillars & Formats

_Read when planning what to post._

## Pillars

### 1. The Origin Story (pin this)
"I built an app with a broken fibula." Quick cuts: injury → recovery → laptop → push-ups → app.
Raw voice-note narration. The hook for everyone who lands on the account.

### 2. Fighter Listicle-Rants (the proven viral engine)
The format that hit 160k and 27k. Numbered, tactical, curiosity-gap hook. See
[viral-format.md](viral-format.md) for the exact structure. Highest priority for growth.

### 3. Talking-Head Rants
Matteo to camera, raw, unfiltered, short. Topics that work:
- What a streetfight taught me that BJJ didn't
- Why your gym is making you soft
- Bangkok Muay Thai vs Western boxing gyms
- Why I log my training even when I'm injured
- "ACL surgery is coming — here's my plan"

### 4. Training-With-Limitations
Push-ups while the leg heals, conditioning around injury. Proves he lives it. Critical for
credibility and endlessly producible during recovery.

### 5. Dogfooding Stance (soft bridge to product)
Show the app naturally — "here's my week in Stance," "my Stance Score dropped, here's why." Never a
hard sell. Always "this is what I use."

## Formats that work
- **Listicle-rant reel** — text overlay + footage, numbered payoff, `READ DESCRIPTION ⬇️`. Batchable.
- **Talking-head rant** — raw lighting, real location, key line as overlay. No stock footage.
- **Training footage** — even just push-ups; the grind as B-roll.
- **Stat/number reveal** — a number fills the screen, then context, then CTA. Very batchable.

## What NOT to make
Generic motivational quotes over stock photos. 8-slide tip carousels (they flop). Anything that
reads wellness/yoga. Pointless "day in my life" with no hook. Over-produced app ads.

## Caption formula
See [voice.md](voice.md) and [viral-format.md](viral-format.md). Short hook line → optional support
line → one CTA → 2–4 niche hashtags.

## Cadence
Consistency over volume — pick a rhythm Matteo can sustain through recovery and batch-produce the
listicle and stat formats. Let Phase 1 analytics confirm best posting days/times.
