# @matteofights — Brand Identity

_Read when designing posts, thumbnails, or the dashboard. This is the @matteofights look & feel —
distinct from the Stance app brand, though they share a combat aesthetic._

## In one line
A real fighter documenting the work — discipline, martial arts, and the injury-to-build story —
in raw, fight-poster-grade content.

## Personality — 3 words
Honest. Disciplined. Unfiltered.

## Visual identity
- **Aesthetic:** fight poster meets training-floor grit. Real footage over stock, always.
- **Palette:** combat **red** `#C82020` + **black** `#0B0B0D`, **gold/amber** `#C49040` for
  wins/PRs/milestones. (Shared with Stance so the worlds feel connected.)
- **Thumbnails:** ALL-CAPS bold text, **red on dark training footage** or black background. No
  gradients, no drop shadows. Poster energy.
- **Typography:** condensed display face for headlines — **Bebas Neue** (also used in the carousel
  renderer). Body/UI: Inter or Geist. Numbers/data: a mono face.

## Verbal identity
See [voice.md](voice.md). Short, blunt, direct address. Talks like an experienced training partner,
never a life coach.

## Dashboard / system aesthetic
The "sick UI" should read like a **dark combat dashboard**: black base, red accents, gold for
highlights, mono numerals, Bebas headers. Premium and serious, not playful. Think fight poster ×
tech dashboard — the same direction as the Stance app.

## What breaks the brand
Pastel/wellness aesthetics, yoga-brand softness, stocky motivational-quote graphics, anything
over-produced enough to feel like an ad. Raw beats polished for this audience.

## Video / Cinematic Look

**Color grade:** Warm-gritty base with punchy contrast. Warm orange-brown skin tones,
deep (not crushed) shadows, slight red push in midtones. Consistent across all cameras.
LUT: `content-bank/luts/matteofights-v1.cube`

**Camera pipeline:** iPhone 13 BM (Blackmagic Film log) + iPhone 15 BM (Apple Log) +
Nikon D750 (Flat profile) → DaVinci grade → CapCut edit.

**Caption style on video:** Three animated styles — Word-Pop (bold, synced to speech),
Title Card (impact moments, red accent word), Lower Third (talking head full sentences).
All animated, nothing static. Colors: white, black, #CC2222 red. Font: Impact / Arial Black.

**Reel formats:** Voiceover + b-roll (primary), Talking head + b-roll (secondary).
Production guides: `production/shooting/`, `production/color/`, `production/editing/`
