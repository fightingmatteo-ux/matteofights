## TWO PROVEN ARCHETYPES (confirmed by 2026-06-05 data)

The account has two distinct viral formats. Both are listicles; they optimize for different signals.

### A) Tactical Listicle → SAVES
Hook: `[NUMBER] WAYS TO [OUTCOME] WITHOUT [REQUIREMENT]`. Numbered, usable tips. Closes with a
save/DM CTA. Hits: 160k (1,937 saves), 32k, 27k, 21k. This is the section above (Reel 1).

### B) Identity Listicle → SHARES / REPOSTS
Hook: `[NUMBER] SIGNS YOU [ARE / AREN'T A REAL FIGHTER]`. Statements about who the viewer *is*, not
tips. Closes with **"Repost this so real fighters find it."** This produced the biggest post ever:

**Rotate A and B.** A keeps the save signal high; B detonates reach via shares.

## FORMAT RULES (never break these)

**Thumbnail:**
- ALL CAPS bold text
- Red text on dark training footage OR dark background
- No gradients, no shadows
- Fight poster energy

**Caption structure:**
1. **Subhook** — same text as the on-screen line under the hook in the video:
   - Identity: `Some people [easy thing]. / Some people [built different].` + `Most fighters never figure out #[N].`
   - Tactical: `You don't need [barrier] to [outcome].` + `Most fighters never figure out #[N].`
   - Point at the most counterintuitive item on the list — don't default to #4, pick the one that earns it
2. "Here are X [ways/things/signs]..." transition line (or skip for identity format)
3. Numbered list: `[number]. [concept] [emoji] [short title] → [elaboration]`
4. Engagement CTA: `Drop a [emoji] if [relatable statement]`
5. Conversion CTA: `DM Me "[WORD]" if [specific offer]`
6. 5 hashtags max — niche specific

**In-video text:**
- Add `READ DESCRIPTION ⬇️` as a text overlay near the end
- This drives caption expansions = more engagement signal

**Hook formula:**
`[NUMBER] WAYS TO [ASPIRATIONAL OUTCOME] WITHOUT [EXPECTED REQUIREMENT]`
The "WITHOUT" creates the curiosity gap.
