Here are 7 ways to hit harder without touching a weight:

1. Drop your shoulder before you throw 💥 Power starts at the hip — the shoulder cues it
2. Exhale sharp on contact ⚡ Force transfers into the target, not back into you
3. Step into the punch, not after it 👣 Timing your weight shift doubles the load without extra muscle
4. Stop punching at the target — punch through it 🔒 Extra 6 inches of extension = different feeling entirely
5. Relax your hand until the last inch 🤫 Tension bleeds speed; speed is power
6. Learn to pivot off your back foot 🔄 Rotation, not arm — this is where knockouts actually come from
7. Fix your stance width 📐 Too wide kills hip rotation; too narrow kills base — find the line
