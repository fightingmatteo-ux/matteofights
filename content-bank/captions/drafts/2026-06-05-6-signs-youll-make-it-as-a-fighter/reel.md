6 signs you'll actually make it as a fighter:

1. You train hardest when nobody's watching
2. You take losses personally, then study them
3. You stay calm in the rounds everyone else panics in
4. You'd rather get humbled than stay comfortable
5. You show up the day after getting beat up
6. You were never chasing motivation, you were chasing a test

Most people want to look like fighters.
Almost nobody is willing to be bad long enough to become one.
