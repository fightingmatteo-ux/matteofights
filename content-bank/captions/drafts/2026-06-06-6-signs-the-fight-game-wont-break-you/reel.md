There's a difference between people who train and people who fight.
Most will never admit which one they are. Especially after #4.

6 signs you're a real fighter:

1. You get hit and your first instinct is to think, not panic
2. Losing bothers you for days. And you use every one of them
3. You show up harder after a bad session, not softer
4. You've been humbled in the gym and came back the next morning
5. You don't train for the videos. You train for the rounds nobody sees
6. You chose the hard sport and you'd choose it again

Most people want to be associated with fighting.
Fighters want to fight.
