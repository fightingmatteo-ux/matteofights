# 7 WAYS TO STOP GETTING HIT IN SPARRING (WITHOUT GETTING FASTER)

## Caption
You don't need faster hands to stop getting hit.
You need to stop making the same 7 mistakes.

Here are 7 ways to stop getting tagged in sparring without getting any faster:

1. Watch the chest, not the hands 👁️ Punches start there — you read shots earlier instead of chasing gloves

2. Make your guard a habit, not a decision 🛡️ Hands glued home between every exchange, no drifting

3. Move your head off the center line 🌀 One small slip after you punch — standing still makes you a target

4. Own the distance with your lead hand 📏 A busy jab and frame keep them at the range you picked

5. Exit on an angle, never straight back ↩️ Straight back = walking into the next shot

6. Breathe out on every punch 💨 Tension makes you slow and readable, relaxed makes you reactive

7. Reset after every combo 🔁 Beginners get clipped on the way out, not the way in

Defense isn't talent. It's habits you drill until they're automatic.
