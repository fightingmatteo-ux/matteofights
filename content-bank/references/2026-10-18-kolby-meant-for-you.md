## Caption (whole thing)

> This is so niche that if you found this, it was meant for you🤝

## What the caption does

- **Selection framing.** "So niche" + "meant for you" tells the viewer the algorithm picked *them*. It's the same identity
  move as our best caption (`Some people train for fun.`), but aimed at the viewer instead of a category of people.
- **Zero instruction, zero CTA.** No list, no "save this", no keyword. The emotional hit has to come from the video.
- **Short.** One line, so all the work happens on screen. That fits Matteo's talking-points style.
