## Caption (whole thing)

> This is so niche that if you found this, it was meant for you🤝

## What the caption does

- **Selection framing.** "So niche" + "meant for you" tells the viewer the algorithm picked *them*. It's the same identity
  move as our best caption (`Some people train for fun.`), but aimed at the viewer instead of a category of people.
- **Zero instruction, zero CTA.** No list, no "save this", no keyword. The emotional hit has to come from the video.
- **Short.** One line, so all the work happens on screen. That fits Matteo's talking-points style.

## The video (98 s, watched)

**Not a combat psychology reel.** It's an identity story: *the psychology of the athlete → entrepreneur → artist arc*.

**Hook, 0–8 s:** a white title card at the top reads `The psychology of the athlete → entrepreneur → artist arc`, with
three photos under it (an athlete with a trophy, a man at a whiteboard, an artist drawing). Kolby talks to camera in
front of a bookshelf with one-word captions. Spoken: "Hear me out, there's a certain type of person who will never be
satisfied with doing just one thing with their life, and they almost always follow a similar path."

**Body, 8–88 s:** one take, three acts, each describing the viewer back to them in second person:
1. **Athlete:** sport → fitness → "you get addicted to the feeling of progress."
2. **Entrepreneur:** "If I can transform my body through discipline and repetition, what else can I do it with?" → books,
   podcasts → "the same principles you learned through sports… apply perfectly to entrepreneurship."
3. **Artist:** "there's another side to you that doesn't fit either world": music, film, philosophy, psychology.

Turn at 73 s: "everyone keeps telling you to pick one thing" → "maybe they're three parts of the same person."
Payoff line: **"An athlete's body, an entrepreneur's mind, an artist's soul."**

**Close:** "If you feel like you've spent your entire life with too many interests to fit into one category, then
you've just found your people." That is a follow CTA dressed up as belonging. A new photo comes up every 2–4 s; there's no
b-roll, just him talking.

### Why it gets ~7% likes per view
- **The viewer is the main character.** It's all "you", and every detail is specific enough to feel personal (sleep,
  energy, podcasts, philosophy). People like it because it describes them.
- **It resolves a tension people feel.** "Pick one thing" vs "I can't" ends as "you're not scattered, you're whole". A
  relief that big gets likes and shares.
- **A tribe close.** "You've just found your people" turns a like into a follow.
