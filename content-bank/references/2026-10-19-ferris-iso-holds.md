## What the caption does

- **"I totally forgot to mention this in the video."** The opener sounds like a correction, which makes it feel candid
  and pushes viewers who already watched to read on. It also leaves out something useful (the sets and reps) on
  purpose, so the caption gives a reason to rewatch and save.
- **The caption is a full program.** The video sells the idea; the caption gives a week split you can follow. That is
  the same split as our `READ DESCRIPTION` end card, but done better: the payoff is something you can copy, not more
  explaining.
- **Coach authority without bragging.** "How I superset them with my team" shows he coaches real fighters without
  saying so outright.
- **A firm limit.** "Don't do more than that. It's unnecessary." A clear rule with a reason (motor unit recruitment)
  reads as expertise. It is also barrier removal by dose (it only takes 15 seconds), which is a different move from
  our spent `You don't need X` line.
