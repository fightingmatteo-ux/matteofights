The Foundation Rules

01
Every session has a target intensity — and it's not always high

UFC coaches assign every session a target RPE (effort out of 10). Off-camp sessions sit at 5–7/10. You shouldn't be destroying yourself every session. An 8 you repeat for months beats a 10 that breaks you in two weeks.

02
Technical drilling before live work. Always.

The UFC PI puts drilling before sparring, every time. A technique must be clean when you're fresh before it's tested under fatigue. If you're always going live before drilling, you're training your bad habits under pressure.

03
Minimum 48 hours between sparring sessions

Non-negotiable in the UFC PI protocol. Sparring accumulates damage — and damage needs time to clear. Off-camp sparring stays at learning intensity. Controlled, not competitive. Hard rounds are for fight camp.

04
Time every single round

3 minutes on, 1 minute off. The only way to train for an actual fight. Untimed bag work builds zero fight-specific conditioning. Set a timer for every bag session — no exceptions.

05
Build before you peak

The UFC PI runs a 4-week cycle: 3 weeks of building load, then 1 deload week. This is how professional fighters avoid injuries and keep progressing for years instead of burning out in months.

WEEK 02 — BUILD
Target RPE 6–7 / 10
Same structure as Week 01. Apply these changes:
Session 01
Add 1 bag round (5 total). Introduce a 3-punch combo of your choice.
Session 02
Add 1 set to each strength exercise. Burpee rounds increase to 7.
Session 03
Add 1 bag round (7 total). Work on whatever you identified from the sparring film review.
WEEK 03 — PEAK
Target RPE 7–8 / 10
Same structure. Apply these changes:
Session 01
6 bag rounds. Add head movement between combos.
Session 02
Conditioning rounds increase to 10. Add bodyweight squats (4 × 15) for leg drive in punching.
Session 03
8 bag rounds. Sparring at Level 2 — you can feel power, not trying to damage. Film it.
WEEK 04 — DELOAD
Target RPE 4–5 / 10
Drop volume by 40%. This is when adaptation locks in — not optional.
Session 01
3 bag rounds only. Technique focus only, no intensity.
Session 02
Strength only, no conditioning. 3 sets each, nothing to failure.
Session 03
Shadowboxing and light drilling only. No sparring.

Session Tracking

After every session, write down two things. Thirty seconds. Compounds over months. This is the method UFC athletes use to self-monitor between coaching sessions.

01
RPE (1–10)

How hard did it actually feel? If it regularly exceeds the target, reduce volume. If it's always below, you have room to push.

02
One Observation

What worked, what didn't, what to fix next session. This is what separates fighters who progress from fighters who plateau.
