Who this is

matteofights.me and the tools behind it are run by Matteo (@matteofights) to publish his own videos. Contact: fightingmatteo@gmail.com.

The publishing tool

"matteofights poster" is a private tool that uploads Matteo's own short videos to his own accounts on YouTube, Facebook, Instagram and Threads. It is not offered to anyone else.

  • It only accesses the accounts Matteo connects himself, with the permissions he grants (for YouTube: uploading videos to his channel).
  • It stores the access tokens needed to post, encrypted, in the tool's private settings. It never sells or shares data about anyone (see below for the little it keeps).
  • Access can be removed at any time in the account's security settings (for Google: myaccount.google.com/permissions).

Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. YouTube uploads are subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

THE PROTOCOL, the STANCE waitlist and Instagram DMs

  • When you sign up for THE PROTOCOL or the STANCE waitlist, we keep the email you give, your answers to the assessment (focus, training frequency, equipment) and, if you add it, your Instagram handle. Matteo uses them to send you the plan and to follow up with you personally. They are stored privately (Cloudflare, Notion) and are never sold or shared.
  • When you comment the keyword PROTOCOL on an @matteofights post, our tool reads that comment and your username through Instagram's official API, replies to the comment and sends you one direct message with the link. It keeps a record of the comment id and username so you are only messaged once.
  • To have your data deleted, email fightingmatteo@gmail.com and it is removed within 30 days.

This website

matteofights.me is a static page. It sets no tracking cookies of its own.
