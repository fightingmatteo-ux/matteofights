Who this is

matteofights.me and the tools behind it are run by Matteo (@matteofights) to publish his own videos. Contact: fightingmatteo@gmail.com.

The publishing tool

"matteofights poster" is a private tool that uploads Matteo's own short videos to his own accounts on YouTube, Facebook, Instagram and Threads. It is not offered to anyone else.

  • It only accesses the accounts Matteo connects himself, with the permissions he grants (for YouTube: uploading videos to his channel).
  • It stores the access tokens needed to post, encrypted, in the tool's private settings. It does not collect, sell or share data about other people.
  • Access can be removed at any time in the account's security settings (for Google: myaccount.google.com/permissions).

Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. YouTube uploads are subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

This website

matteofights.me is a static page. It sets no tracking cookies of its own.
