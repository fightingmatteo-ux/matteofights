# Effects shots: how to film them so Claude can edit them

From the nvision.media breakdown (`content-bank/references/2026-10-23-nvision-effects-breakdown.md`).
Each card: the setup, what to do, the mistake that kills it, then the @matteofights and the Gypsy Amazon version.
The effects themselves Claude builds in the cloud (subject matte with `reel-kit/editorial/cutout.py`, HyperFrames).
Your job on set is to give the edit clean material. Most "editing skill" is decided here.

## Settings for every effects shot
- Blackmagic Camera, same as `iphone-15-bm.md`, but **4K** for effects shots (we crop, clone and reframe; 1080p
  falls apart). 24 fps, shutter 1/48. Slow-mo shots: 60 fps (1/120) or 120 fps (1/240).
- **Lock everything** before rolling: exposure, focus, white balance. A shot that pumps exposure cannot be cloned or
  layered.
- Vertical (9:16) unless the card says otherwise. Keep the subject's head below the top 15 % (captions live there).
- **Separation:** subject against a plain, brighter or contrasting background. The matte is only as clean as this.
  Bangkok tip: sky, a white wall, a shutter door. Avoid busy gym racks behind your head.
- Roll 2 s before and 2 s after every action. Say the card number out loud at the start ("five, take two").
- One location, one outfit, one prop per video. That is what makes it look like one film.

---

## 1. Masking hook (shapes on black, then the reveal)
- **Setup:** tripod or very steady hand, waist-up or tighter, plain background.
- **Do:** one 3 s shot, a clear pose, one signature prop that has its own shape (the sunglasses in the reference).
  Small move at the end (look up into lens).
- **Kills it:** prop hidden behind hands, background the same colour as your clothes.
- **Matteo:** gloves up by the chin, or a mouthguard going in. Edit: body, then head, then just the gloves flash
  white on black on the beat, then the colour shot hits with the hook text. Frame 0 still shows the hook text.
- **Gypsy Amazon:** the product is the prop. Bag on the shoulder, earrings, a hat. Edit: outline of the model,
  then only the product glows, then the reveal. The product is the last shape before colour.

## 2. Wipe the frame (hand into lens, out into a wide)
Two shots filmed as a pair.
- **Shot A:** medium shot, then push a hand / glove / object straight into the lens until it fills the frame
  (it will blur, that is the point). Move fast, left to right.
- **Shot B:** start with the same thing covering the lens, pull it away in the SAME direction, opening on a **wide**
  (hero shot: the whole place, you small in it).
- **Kills it:** different direction or speed in A and B; B is not a wide.
- **Matteo:** jab straight at the lens, then open on the empty ring / the gym from the doorway, you standing alone.
- **Gypsy Amazon:** fabric (a scarf, a dress hem) swept across the lens, opening on the full outfit or the shop
  shelf.

## 3 + 4. Layer effect + sound wave (echo trails that pulse on the bass)
- **Setup:** tripod, locked, nothing else moving in frame.
- **Do:** 4-6 s of slow, big movement in one place: turning around, a slow head turn, arms raised.
  Then walk out of frame fast (Claude smears that into the next shot).
- **Kills it:** camera moves even slightly (the copies smear the background too); fast movement (copies become noise).
- **Matteo:** slow-motion shadowboxing in place, or turning from the bag to the camera.
- **Gypsy Amazon:** model turning on the spot in a flowy piece. The echoes show the fabric moving.

## 5. Clone (three of you in one frame)
The highest-value shot here. Needs patience, not skill.
- **Setup:** tripod, locked, 4K, wide enough for 3 people. Do not touch the phone between takes (use the
  Blackmagic remote / Apple Watch, or have someone press record).
- **Do:** first record 5 s of the **empty frame** (the plate). Then one take per position: left, centre, right.
  Do not cross into another take's zone. Same lighting: film all takes within ~10 minutes (sun moves).
- **Kills it:** bumping the tripod, auto exposure on, clouds rolling over between takes.
- **Matteo:** the 3 versions of you before a fight: one wraps hands on the bench, one shadowboxes, one sits still
  staring at the floor. Identity + contrast in one frame, the format that wins for us.
- **Gypsy Amazon:** "one piece, three ways": the same model in 3 outfits styled around one product. Pure ecommerce.

## 6. Flash (one-frame white silhouette on a hit)
- Nothing to film. Claude makes it from any clean subject shot (cards 1, 5, 7). Just give clean separation.
- **Matteo:** a white silhouette flash on every punch that lands in a pad round.
- **Gypsy Amazon:** flash on the product on each beat of a reveal.

## 7. Silhouette overlay (your head filled with other clips)
- **Setup:** profile or three-quarter, face against **bright open sky** (backlit), camera slightly low.
  Expose for the sky so you go almost black.
- **Do:** hold still 3-4 s. Then a slow head turn, 3 s.
- **Kills it:** trees or buildings behind the head, front light on your face.
- **Matteo:** psych lane in one image: your silhouette filled with your fight clips / training b-roll, "what you
  replay before you sleep". Use on a quote-card line.
- **Gypsy Amazon:** model silhouette filled with the collection, the travel / location shots, or textures.

## 8. Lyric pop
- Nothing to film. Our editorial keyword captions do this. Leave a clean wide (sky, empty wall) for the word.

## 9. Slow-mo breather
- 120 fps, 5 s, something with texture moving: water, sweat flicking off, chalk, wraps unrolling, fabric.
- **Matteo:** silent b-roll only, never on your voice (you cut slow-mo from the Saenchai edit). Max one per reel.
- **Gypsy Amazon:** use freely, it sells fabric and movement.

## 10. Handheld movement
- Phone low at chest height or selfie arm, walk, sky or ceiling behind you, keep the horizon roughly level.
  Use Blackmagic stabilisation on. 5-8 s per shot.
- **Matteo:** walking into the gym, down the street to training, the hallway before a fight.
- **Gypsy Amazon:** behind the scenes: packing orders, walking the market, picking stock.

## 11. Inverted / black-and-white shot
- Same as card 7 (backlit, black subject on white sky), but with movement: walking past, raising a fist.
  Claude does the B&W, the contrast and the shape-mask wipe.
- **Matteo:** fits the editorial look (black, white, red keyword).

## Closer: wide walk + logo
- Wide, locked, subject walks across the full frame slowly, 8 s, leave the top half empty (logo goes there).
- Matteo: needs a simple MATTEOFIGHTS wordmark. Gypsy Amazon: their logo file (PNG with transparency).

## How the two brands stay different
| | @matteofights | Gypsy Amazon |
|---|---|---|
| Subject | Matteo, fighter, real training | The product, on a model |
| Effects per reel | 2-4, hook + keywords only | A full run is fine (fashion/product audiences expect it) |
| Look | Editorial: neutral colour, white/black, red `#E8112D` keyword, Geist captions | **Ask:** brand colours, font, logo |
| Slow-mo | Silent b-roll, max 1 | Freely |
| Signature effect | Clone ("3 versions of you"), silhouette overlay | Clone ("one piece, three ways"), masking hook on the product |

## First test shoot (about 40 minutes)
Film cards **1, 2 (A+B), 5 (plate + 3 takes), 7, 10**. Upload to the Drive input folder (`PIPELINE.md` step 3) or
the `media` release, tell Claude which card each file is. Claude builds each effect, sends the results back, and
marks on each one what to change next time.
