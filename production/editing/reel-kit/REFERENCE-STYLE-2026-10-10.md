# @matteofights Talking-Head Reel — Edit Style Spec

Goal of every reel: **Stance waitlist sign-ups + followers.**
Pipeline: raw 9:16 talking head → Whisper (word timestamps) → HyperFrames composition → render → queue IG + FB.

## Taken from the references

| Ref | Keep | Beat it on |
|---|---|---|
| coachkyle "Weapon-Maxxing" | Top-band image overlay with a yellow keyword label; persistent series title; a new visual every 2–4s | Captions (tiny, static, centered on the chest), no motion, stills only |
| thepunchdoctor "natural puncher" | B-roll that *demonstrates* the line (drawn red motion arcs on the body, fight footage, graph of acceleration, solid-color cutaway with one word); keyword CTA "Comment NATURAL"; last line loops into the first | Low resolution, flat single-word caption style, no transitions |
| natethemachine "3 fighter quotes" | Hook = listicle promise + triptych of famous fighters + title in frame 1; music bed under voice | Captions plain, overlay just cuts, no SFX |

All three keep music ~20–30 dB under the voice.

## Rules

**Hook (0–2s)** — never the logo. Frame 1 already has: the title promise on screen, a top-band visual, and a punch-in on the face; impact SFX on the first word. If the strongest line is mid-video, pull it to the front.

**Captions** — 2–4 words per group, word-by-word reveal synced to Whisper timestamps. Bold condensed display face, ALL CAPS, ivory `#F2F0EA`; the active/emphasis word turns signal red `#C82020`/`#FF1E1E` with a scale pop; numbers in Geist Mono. Placement: lower-middle, clear of IG UI safe zones. Keyword hits (e.g. "DISCIPLINE", "ENVIRONMENT") get a full-width slam moment, max 1 every ~8s.

**Overlays / b-roll** — top-band panel (ref 1) for concepts, full-screen b-roll cutaways (ref 2) for demonstrations, red line annotations drawn over motion, stat/number cards in Geist Mono. New visual every 2–4s.

**Brand** — red/black/ivory, fight poster meets tech dashboard. No gradients, glow, 3D, flames, gloves-and-fists clichés. Stance logo only at the end as the payoff.

**Audio** — music: hip hop / Brazilian funk (phonk-adjacent) bed, ducked under voice; trending sounds added natively in the IG app at post time when licensing requires it. SFX: hit, riser, whoosh, digital click (existing set in `pre-scheduling/SFX`).

**CTA** — keyword comment CTA: "Comment STANCE and I'll send you the waitlist" (+ follow prompt). On-screen in the last 3s.

**Loop** — last sentence written/cut to flow into the first word; final frame matches first frame's framing, caption state, and audio phrase so the replay is seamless.
