A kick that gets caught is a free sweep.
A kick that lands changes the fight.

7 ways to throw kicks that don't get caught:

1. Set it up with your hands first
2. Kick through the target, don't push it
3. Bring the leg back as fast as it went out
4. Kick right after they punch, when their weight is forward
5. Go low before you go high
6. Turn the hip over so it lands with the shin
7. Don't step out wide first, it tells them it's coming
