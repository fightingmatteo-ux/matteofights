The ropes feel safe.
They're the worst place in the ring.

6 ways to get off the ropes when you're trapped:

1. Pivot out to the side, never straight back along the ropes
2. Throw a short shot first so they stop for a second
3. Duck under and roll to the open side
4. Clinch, then turn them onto the ropes
5. Keep your elbows tight while you look for the exit
6. Watch their lead foot, it tells you which side is open
