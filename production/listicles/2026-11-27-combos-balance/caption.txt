Long combos look great.
Until you're off balance at the end of them.

6 ways to throw combos without losing your balance:

1. Keep your feet under your shoulders
2. Step in instead of reaching
3. Throw two or three punches, not six
4. Let each punch set your weight for the next
5. Finish with a step out or a slip
6. Drill them slowly on the bag first
