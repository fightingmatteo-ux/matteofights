Some people run miles they'll never fight.
Some people train the way fights actually go.

Here are 8 ways to get fight fit without running every morning:

1. Rounds, not miles ⏱️ 3 on, 1 off → train the rhythm of a fight

2. Bag sprints 🥊 30 seconds all-out, 30 easy → fights are bursts, not jogs

3. Skip rope 🪢 10 minutes → feet and lungs at once

4. Clinch rounds 🔒 2 minutes of pummelling → the most tiring position in the sport

5. Nose breathing 💨 Easy work through the nose → you recover faster between rounds

6. Hill sprints ⛰️ Short and brutal, once a week → power that lasts

7. Spar short and fast 🎯 Shorter rounds, higher pace → a gas tank that matters

8. Sleep 😴 8 hours → the conditioning nobody films
