Coaches say chin down a thousand times.
Then you get hit and remember.

6 ways to keep your chin down without thinking about it:

1. Shadowbox with a tennis ball under your chin
2. Look through your eyebrows, not straight ahead
3. Bring your shoulder up when you punch
4. Keep it down on the bag, not just in sparring
5. Get corrected on it every session until it sticks
6. Check it in the mirror between rounds
