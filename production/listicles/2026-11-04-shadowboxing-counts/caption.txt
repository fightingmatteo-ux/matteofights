Most shadowboxing is dancing.
Good shadowboxing is a fight with nobody there.

6 ways to shadowbox so it actually counts:

1. Picture a real opponent and their habits
2. Defend after every combo, not just attack
3. Move your feet with every punch
4. Work one thing per round
5. Go at fight speed for the last 30 seconds
6. Watch yourself in a mirror once a week
