Busy weeks don't kill training.
All-or-nothing does.

How people who train stay consistent when life gets busy:

1. A short session still counts
2. Train at the same time every day
3. Bag packed the night before
4. Shadowbox at home when you can't get to the gym
5. Plan the week on Sunday
6. Never miss twice in a row
