Head shots hurt.
Liver shots end things.

6 ways to land a liver shot:

1. Step to your left so you're lined up with it
2. Bend your knees, don't just drop your hand
3. Throw it after a jab to the head
4. Keep your right hand on your chin while you throw
5. Turn your hip into it like a hook upstairs
6. Go back to the head right after
