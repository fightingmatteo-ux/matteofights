The clinch drains you fastest.
Getting out is technique, not strength.

6 ways to escape the clinch when you're tired:

1. Get your hands inside theirs first
2. Frame on their neck or shoulder to make space
3. Turn your hips, don't just pull back
4. Step back and push off at the same time
5. Knee as you leave so they let go
6. Breathe while you're locked up, don't hold it
