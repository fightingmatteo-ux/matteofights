Your cardio isn't the problem on fight night.
The adrenaline dump is.

6 ways to stop gassing out in your first fight:

1. Warm up to a light sweat, not until you're tired
2. Breathe out slow through your nose on the walk out
3. Throw your first combo early to burn off the jitters
4. Drop your shoulders between exchanges
5. Stop chasing the knockout in round one
6. Spar some rounds at fight pace during camp
