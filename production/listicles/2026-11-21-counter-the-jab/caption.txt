The jab sets everything up.
So make it the most dangerous punch they throw.

6 ways to counter the jab:

1. Slip outside and throw the right hand
2. Parry it and jab back straight away
3. Step back and land your own jab as theirs pulls back
4. Slip inside and go to the body
5. Lean back and counter with the right
6. Time it: once they're predictable, beat it with yours
