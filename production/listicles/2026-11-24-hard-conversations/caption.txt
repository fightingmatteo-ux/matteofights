A hard round and a hard talk feel the same.
You get better at both the same way.

6 things training teaches you about hard conversations:

1. Breathe before you answer
2. Don't flinch, stay in it
3. Say the hard thing early, not after round five
4. Listen like you're reading an opponent
5. Stay calm when it gets heated
6. Shake hands after, you'll see them again

Follow for the fighter's side of everything else.
