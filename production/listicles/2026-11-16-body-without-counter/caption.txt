Going to the body opens your head.
Unless you do it right.

6 ways to hit the body without getting countered:

1. Bend your knees to go low, don't drop your head forward
2. Keep the other hand glued to your chin
3. Go to the body after a jab upstairs
4. Step to the side as you throw
5. Throw one shot, then come back up
6. Finish with a shot upstairs so they can't settle
