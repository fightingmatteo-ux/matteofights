Size matters in the clinch.
Position matters more.

8 ways to handle a bigger guy in the clinch:

1. Get your hands inside before he locks his
2. Keep your hips close so he can't throw you around
3. Turn him instead of pushing him
4. Use your elbow to make space when you need it
5. Knee to the body the moment his posture breaks
6. Don't fight his strength, move his head
7. Break off on your terms, not his
8. Stay calm, a tired big man is a slow big man
