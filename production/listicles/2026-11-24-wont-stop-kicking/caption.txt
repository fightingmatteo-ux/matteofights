Kickers win at long range.
So take the long range away.

6 ways to fight someone who won't stop kicking:

1. Close the distance right after they kick
2. Check the leg kicks so they cost them
3. Catch the body kick and sweep or punch back
4. Punch as they kick, a straight beats a slow kick
5. Kick their standing leg
6. Crowd them so they have no room to kick
