Eat enough leg kicks and your stance falls apart.
Checking is a habit, not a reflex.

6 ways to check leg kicks that actually work:

1. Lift the knee toward your elbow, not out to the side
2. Turn the shin out a little to meet the kick
3. Keep your weight on the back leg so you can lift
4. Drill it with a partner, light, every session
5. Step back when you can't check in time
6. Kick back straight away so they think twice
