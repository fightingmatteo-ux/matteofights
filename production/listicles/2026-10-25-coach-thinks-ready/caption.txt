Some people ask when they can fight.
Some people get told.

6 signs your coach thinks you're ready to fight:

1. They stop correcting the small stuff
2. They put you in with the people who actually compete
3. They start asking how much you weigh
4. Pad rounds get longer and harder
5. They watch your sparring instead of their phone
6. They ask what you're doing the weekend of the next show
