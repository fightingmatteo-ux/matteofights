Progress in fighting is quiet.
You notice it months later.

7 signs you're improving even if it doesn't feel like it:

1. The shots that used to land now just touch your gloves
2. You see the punch coming instead of feeling it
3. Round three doesn't scare you anymore
4. New people ask you for advice
5. You get bored of drills you used to struggle with
6. Your bad days look like your old good days
7. Your coach yells at you about details, not basics
