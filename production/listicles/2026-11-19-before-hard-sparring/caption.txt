Most people think about the session.
The day before decides how it goes.

What to eat the day before hard sparring:

1. A proper dinner with rice or potatoes
2. Protein in every meal
3. Water all day, not all at once
4. Nothing new or heavy the night before
5. A light meal two or three hours before you spar
6. Salt and fruit if you sweat a lot
