Train on nothing and you gas in round two.
Eat a buffet and you see it again on the pads.

6 rules for eating before a morning session:

1. Eat 60 to 90 minutes before, not 10
2. Oats or rice, something your stomach knows
3. A bit of protein, eggs or yogurt
4. Fruit if you only have 30 minutes
5. Water with a pinch of salt
6. Nothing greasy, nothing new

Save this before your next early session.
