Most people train their hands.
Their feet decide if the hands ever land.

6 footwork drills that make you harder to hit:

1. Line drill: step in and out on a line, stay in stance
2. Pivot off the lead foot, 20 times each side
3. One round of shadowboxing with steps only, no punches
4. Mirror drill: copy a partner's feet for a round
5. Agility ladder in your stance, not running
6. Cut off an imaginary ring, never cross your feet
