Some people spar to win.
Some people spar to find out.

6 ways to learn more from every sparring round:

1. Pick one thing to work on before the bell
2. Film one round a week and watch it the same night
3. Ask your partner what you do every single time
4. After each round, say out loud what caught you
5. Spar people better than you, at half speed
6. Write it in your notes app before you leave
