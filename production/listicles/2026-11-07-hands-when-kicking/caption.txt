6 ways to stop dropping your hands when you kick:

1. Keep the same-side hand by your chin
2. Swing the opposite arm down only, not both
3. Kick on the pads with a partner throwing back
4. Put your hands back before your foot lands
5. Kick slower until your hands stay up
6. Film one round and count how often they drop
