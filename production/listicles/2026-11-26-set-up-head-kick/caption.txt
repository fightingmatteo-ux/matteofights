Thrown cold, a head kick gets blocked.
Set up, it ends rounds.

6 ways to set up a head kick:

1. Kick the body a few times first
2. Land the right hand, then kick the same side
3. Kick when they lean or step in
4. No wind up, keep it fast
5. Turn your hip all the way over
6. Bring it back to stance with your hands up
