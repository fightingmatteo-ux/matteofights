Most people just survive the clinch.
Good clinchers score in it.

6 ways to use your knees in the clinch:

1. Get your posture tall first
2. Control the head before you knee
3. Pull them onto the knee, don't just lift it
4. Knee the body when they lean back
5. Turn them off balance between knees
6. Keep your hips close so they can't knee back
