Some people wait for a partner.
Some people get dangerous alone.

Here are 8 ways to get dangerous without a training partner:

1. Shadowbox with intent 🥊 Fight a real opponent in your head → every round has a plan

2. Mirror work 🪞 Check your guard on every shot → bad habits die alone

3. Bag rounds with a rule 🎯 Only counters this round → you build reactions, not just punches

4. Footwork every day 👣 10 minutes → angles become automatic

5. Study your tape 🎥 Yours next to a pro's → you see the gap

6. Slip line drills 🔁 A rope and your head → defence without a partner

7. Train at fight pace ⏱️ 3-minute rounds, 1 minute rest → your body learns the rhythm

8. Visualise the fight 🧠 Round by round, before bed → your mind gets the reps too
