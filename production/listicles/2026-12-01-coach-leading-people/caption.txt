Good coaches don't shout the most.
They notice the most.

6 things a good coach teaches you about leading people:

1. Fix one thing at a time
2. Praise in front of people, correct in private
3. Hold the pads at their level, not yours
4. Notice when someone's off before they say it
5. Show it, don't just say it
6. Stay after for the ones who want more

Follow for the fighter's side of everything else.
