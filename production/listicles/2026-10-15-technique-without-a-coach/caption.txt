Some people wait for someone to fix them.
Some people fix themselves.

Here are 7 ways to build real technique without a coach:

1. Film every session 🎥 → you can't fix what you can't see

2. Copy one fighter 🧠 Pick one style and steal it → depth beats variety

3. Slow it down 🐢 Half speed, perfect form → speed comes from clean reps

4. One fix per week 🎯 Just the guard. Just the hips → small fixes stack

5. Drill the boring stuff 🔁 Jab, step, reset → the basics win fights

6. Ask the best guy in the gym 💬 One question a session → most will help

7. Test it live 🥊 Sparring is the exam → if it breaks under pressure, it isn't yours yet
