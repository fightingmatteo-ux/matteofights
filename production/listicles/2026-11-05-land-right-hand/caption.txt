Most right hands get blocked.
Set-up ones don't.

7 ways to land your right hand more often:

1. Double the jab first so they expect a third
2. Throw it to the body first, then upstairs
3. Step off to your left before you throw
4. Throw it right after they jab
5. Feint the jab, then throw the right
6. Turn your hip and back foot, not just the arm
7. Throw it less, so it surprises them
