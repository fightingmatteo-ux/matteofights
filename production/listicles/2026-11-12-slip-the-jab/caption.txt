Blocking the jab keeps you safe.
Slipping it gets you paid.

6 ways to slip the jab and make them pay:

1. Slip outside the jab, not inside
2. Move your head just past the glove, no further
3. Bend at the knees, not the waist
4. Throw your right hand as you slip
5. Slip once, then make them miss again
6. Drill it slow with a partner before you try it fast
