Most people use the front kick to push.
Thais use it to break your rhythm.

6 ways to use the teep (Muay Thai push kick):

1. Teep the hip the moment they step in
2. Teep the thigh to stop their kick before it starts
3. Fake the teep to land the cross
4. Teep off the back foot when they pressure you
5. Keep your hands up while you kick
6. Put the foot back in stance fast so you don't get swept
