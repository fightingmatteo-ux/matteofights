Backing up isn't losing.
Backing up straight is.

6 ways to fight off the back foot:

1. Pivot off the jab instead of stepping back
2. Counter as they step in, not after
3. Keep your back foot light so you can move
4. Circle toward their back hand, away from their power
5. Use the teep or a long jab to make space
6. Plant and fire when they over-commit
