Running from pressure gets you trapped on the ropes.
Angles get you out.

7 ways to beat a pressure fighter:

1. Pivot off the jab instead of backing up
2. Step off at an angle, never straight back
3. Land the jab or teep as they step in
4. Make them pay for walking forward with an uppercut
5. Frame and turn them in the clinch
6. Get back to the middle of the ring every time
7. Fight them on your terms for ten seconds, then move
