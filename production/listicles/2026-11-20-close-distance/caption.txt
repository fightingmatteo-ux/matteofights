Walking straight in gets you counted.
Getting in takes a plan.

6 ways to close the distance without getting hit:

1. Come in behind your jab
2. Feint first, then step
3. Step in at an angle, not a straight line
4. Close right after they punch, while they reset
5. Keep your guard tight on the way in
6. Go to the body first, then up
