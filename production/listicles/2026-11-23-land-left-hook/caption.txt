Most left hooks are thrown too wide.
The ones that land are short.

6 ways to land your left hook:

1. Throw it right after the jab, same hand
2. Keep it short, elbow at shoulder height
3. Turn your front foot and hip with it
4. Throw it when they step to your left
5. Go to the body first, then hook upstairs
6. Hook off a slip to your left
