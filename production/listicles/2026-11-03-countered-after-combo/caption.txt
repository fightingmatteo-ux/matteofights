Landing the combo feels good.
Eating the counter after it doesn't.

6 ways to stop getting countered after your combo:

1. Finish on a hook or a kick, not a lazy jab
2. Hands back to your face, not your waist
3. Move your head off the line after the last shot
4. Step out at an angle when you're done
5. Stop admiring the shot that landed
6. End on a feint so they hesitate
