Switch hitters want you confused.
Keep it simple and they lose the edge.

6 ways to handle someone who keeps switching stance:

1. Watch their lead foot, not their hands
2. Circle away from their power hand every time
3. Jab as they switch, they're off balance
4. Have one plan for orthodox, one for southpaw
5. Kick the lead leg, whichever it is
6. Don't switch with them
