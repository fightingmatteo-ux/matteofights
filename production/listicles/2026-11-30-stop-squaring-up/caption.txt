Squared up, you're a bigger target.
Side on, you're harder to hit.

6 ways to stop squaring up when you punch:

1. Keep your rear heel off the floor
2. Pivot the back foot, not both feet
3. Bring the hand back on the same line
4. End every combo in your stance, not facing them
5. Step the lead foot slightly outside after you throw
6. Check your stance in the mirror between rounds
