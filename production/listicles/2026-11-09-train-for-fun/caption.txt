Some people train to compete.

6 signs you train for fun and still take it seriously:

1. You'll never fight, but you still drill the jab
2. You show up on time because the warm-up matters
3. You ask the coach the annoying technical questions
4. You watch fights to steal things, not for the knockouts
5. You care about getting better, not about winning sparring
6. You'd still train if nobody ever saw it
