Everyone blinks at first.
The ones who improve learn to watch.

7 ways to stop blinking when punches come:

1. Have a partner throw slow jabs at your gloves while you watch them land
2. Keep your eyes on the chest, not the gloves
3. Breathe out as the punch comes, not in
4. Let light shots touch your guard on purpose
5. Roll with slow punches before you try to slip fast ones
6. Shadowbox with your eyes open on one spot
7. Do it every session for a month, not once
