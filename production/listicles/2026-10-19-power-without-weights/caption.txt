Some people chase power in the weight room.
Some people find it in their hips.

Here are 8 ways to hit harder without lifting a single weight:

1. Turn the back foot 👟 Power starts on the floor → no pivot, no punch

2. Relax until impact 💨 Loose arm, tight fist at the end → tension kills speed

3. Exhale sharp 🌬️ On every shot → your core locks at the right moment

4. Sit down on it ⬇️ Drop your weight an inch → body behind the punch

5. Punch through the target 🎯 Aim behind the bag → don't stop at the surface

6. Med ball throws 🔥 Rotational slams → explosive hips without a barbell

7. Short shots 🔨 Close range, no wind-up → they don't see it coming

8. Timing over strength ⏱️ Catch them stepping in → the hardest shot is the one they walk into
