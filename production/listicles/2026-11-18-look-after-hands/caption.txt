Your hands are your tools.
Most people only notice them when they hurt.

6 ways fighters look after their hands:

1. Wrap every session, even on light days
2. Use bag gloves heavy enough for the bag
3. Make a tight fist on impact, loose in between
4. Build up bag rounds slowly after a break
5. Dry your gloves and wraps after every session
6. Rest a sore hand before it becomes an injury
