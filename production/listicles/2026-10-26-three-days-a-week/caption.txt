Some people train every day and stay the same.
Some people train three times and get better.

7 ways to improve fast when you can only train 3 times a week:

1. Pick one thing to fix every week and only that
2. Show up five minutes early and shadowbox it
3. Ask the coach one question per session
4. Film one round and watch it at home
5. Shadowbox 10 minutes on the days you can't go
6. Sleep like it's part of training, because it is
7. Never skip the session you least want to do

Save this for your next training week.
