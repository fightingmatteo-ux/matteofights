Counter punchers want you to lead.
So make them lead instead.

6 ways to beat a counter puncher:

1. Feint more than you punch
2. Throw single shots, not long combos
3. Double up the jab to break their timing
4. Attack the body, it's harder to counter
5. Step back and let them come to you
6. Change your rhythm every round
