Some people reward hard days with junk.
Fighters refuel for the next one.

What fighters eat after a hard sparring day:

1. Protein within a couple of hours
2. Rice or potatoes to refill what you burned
3. Water and salt before anything else
4. Fruit for something quick
5. A real dinner, not a shake and nothing
6. Enough that you wake up ready, not wrecked
