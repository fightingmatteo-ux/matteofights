7 ways to keep your hands up when you're exhausted:

1. Rest your gloves on your cheekbones, not in front of your face
2. Relax your shoulders so your arms aren't holding all the weight
3. Breathe out on every shot
4. Throw less when you're tired, not wider
5. Shadowbox the last round of every session with hands glued up
6. Do shoulder work after training, not before
7. Have a partner tap your head every time they drop
