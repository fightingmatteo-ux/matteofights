Most people hit the bag.
Few people train on it.

6 ways to get more out of a bag session:

1. Move around the bag like it's a person
2. Throw combos, then defend and reset
3. Work one thing per round
4. Hit hard on purpose, not every punch
5. Keep your hands up when you're tired
6. Finish with a round at full pace
