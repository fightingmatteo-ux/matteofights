Round one is about skill.
Round three is about decisions.

6 ways to survive round three when your cardio is gone:

1. Breathe out on every strike
2. Use the jab to buy yourself time
3. Clinch to reset instead of running
4. Step off at an angle, never straight back
5. Throw less and make it land
6. Drop your shoulders between exchanges, not your hands
