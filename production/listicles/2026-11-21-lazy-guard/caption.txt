Nobody drops their hands on purpose.
They drop them when they get tired.

6 ways to fix a lazy guard:

1. Touch your cheek after every punch in shadowboxing
2. Ask your partner to tap you when your hands drop
3. Train shoulders so your arms don't give up
4. Bring the hand back the same way it went out
5. Check your guard in the mirror between rounds
6. Do the last round on the bag with hands glued up
