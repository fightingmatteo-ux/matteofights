Getting caught once is part of sparring.
Getting caught by the same thing all round is a habit.

7 ways to stop getting hit with the same shot:

1. Notice what you were doing right before it landed
2. Bring the hand back on the same line it went out
3. Move your head off the centre line after you throw
4. Stop stepping straight back into their range
5. Ask your partner after the round what they kept seeing
6. Drill the defence for that one shot for a week
7. Make them pay once for throwing it

Save this before your next sparring day.
