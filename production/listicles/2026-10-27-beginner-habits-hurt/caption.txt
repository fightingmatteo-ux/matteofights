Hard sparring feels like progress.

7 sparring habits that get beginners hurt:

1. Looking at the floor after you get hit
2. Turning your back when the pressure comes
3. Swinging wide when you're tired
4. Holding your breath until the round ends
5. Leaning in with your chin out
6. Never checking a single kick
7. Going hard with someone 15 kilos heavier
