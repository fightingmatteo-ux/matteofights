One jab is bad luck.
Ten of the same jab is a habit.

6 ways to stop eating the same jab all round:

1. Keep your rear hand up by your cheek to catch it
2. Move your head off the center line after you punch
3. Step off to the side instead of straight back
4. Parry it down and answer with your own jab
5. Slip outside and counter over the top
6. Watch their shoulder, the jab starts there
