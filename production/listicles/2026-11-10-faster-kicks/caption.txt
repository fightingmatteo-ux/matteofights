Slow kicks get caught.
Fast kicks get respect.

6 ways to make your kicks faster:

1. Kick without a wind-up, straight from your stance
2. Turn the standing foot early
3. Throw the arm down to rotate faster
4. Relax until the moment of impact
5. Kick lighter in sparring so speed comes first
6. Do fast single kicks on the bag, not tired ones
