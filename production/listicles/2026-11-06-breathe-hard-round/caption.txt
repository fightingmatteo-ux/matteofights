Most people hold their breath when they get hit.
Then they wonder why they gas.

6 ways to breathe through a hard round:

1. Breathe out on every strike you throw
2. Breathe out when you get hit, don't hold it
3. Breathe through your nose between exchanges
4. Drop your shoulders, tension burns air
5. Slow the pace for ten seconds when you're flat
6. Train rounds at sparring pace so it's normal
