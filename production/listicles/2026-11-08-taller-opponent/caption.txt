Reach wins at range.
Range is something you can take away.

6 ways to deal with a taller opponent:

1. Close the distance behind a feint, not in a straight line
2. Work the body, it's closer than the head
3. Stay off the end of their jab
4. Make them reach, then counter
5. Clinch when you get inside
6. Kick the lead leg to slow their movement
