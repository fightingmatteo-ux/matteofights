Round one isn't about winning.
It's about finding out.

6 ways to read your opponent in the first round:

1. Watch which foot he leads with when he steps in
2. Throw a jab and see how he defends it
3. Notice what he does every time he gets hit
4. Check if he drops a hand when he kicks
5. See if he backs up straight or circles
6. Find the shot he throws when he panics
