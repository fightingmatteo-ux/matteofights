Most people use the jab to touch.
Good fighters use it to hit.

6 ways to make your jab actually hurt:

1. Step in with it, don't just reach
2. Turn the shoulder up and the fist over at the end
3. Push off the back foot, not just the arm
4. Aim through the target, not at it
5. Snap it back as fast as it went out
6. Double it up when they start reading the first one
