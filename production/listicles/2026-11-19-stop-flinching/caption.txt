Flinching isn't weakness.
It's a habit you haven't trained out yet.

6 ways to stop flinching when you get hit:

1. Let light shots land on your guard on purpose
2. Keep your chin down so hits land on your forehead
3. Breathe out when the punch lands
4. Keep your eyes on their chest, not their gloves
5. Answer every hit with a punch back
6. Spar light and often, not hard and rarely
