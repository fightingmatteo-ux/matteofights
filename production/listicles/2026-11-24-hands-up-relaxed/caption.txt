Tense arms tire in one round.
Relaxed arms last the whole fight.

6 ways to keep your hands up without tensing up:

1. Let the gloves rest on your face, not hover
2. Drop your shoulders
3. Breathe out slowly between exchanges
4. Squeeze only when the punch lands
5. Shadowbox with light weights in your hands, briefly
6. Build shoulder endurance, not just strength
