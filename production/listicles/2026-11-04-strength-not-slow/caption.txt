Some people lift like bodybuilders.
Fighters lift so they can still move in round five.

How fighters get stronger without getting slow:

1. Pull-ups and push-ups before any machine
2. Low reps, fast reps, never to failure
3. Lift on the same day as hard sparring so rest days stay rest
4. Train the neck and the core, not the mirror muscles
5. Stop the set when the speed drops
