Everyone looks sharp on pads.
Most of it disappears in sparring.

6 ways to make your pad work show up in sparring:

1. Ask your holder to hit back after every combo
2. Move your feet between combos like it's a round
3. Defend before you attack, every time
4. Drill the two combos you actually use
5. Do the last round at sparring pace
6. Try one pad combo in every spar that week
