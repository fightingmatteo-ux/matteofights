The first minute feels like forever.

6 ways to survive your first sparring session:

1. Tell your partner it's your first time
2. Keep your hands up even when you're tired
3. Breathe out on every punch you throw
4. Don't try to win, try to see
5. Go back to your jab when you panic
6. Come back next week, that's the whole secret
