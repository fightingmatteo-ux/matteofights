Naoya Inoue learned boxing from his father.
He still fights on the basics.

3 things behind the Monster:

1. The jab. Fast, from the edge of range. It measures you and hides everything behind it.
2. The body shot. He digs to the body early, so by the middle rounds your guard is already coming down.
3. Balance. His weight is over his feet on every punch, so his whole body goes into it, even the ones that don't look big.

Want more power? Don't swing harder. Get your feet under you first.
