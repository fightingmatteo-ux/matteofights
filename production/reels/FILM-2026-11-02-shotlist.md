# Shot list: what's on screen while Matteo talks (2026-11-02 plan reels)

Matteo's rule: **the picture shows exactly what the words say.** Overlays only (his voice is one take, never cut).
Pro clips 2 to 4 s, picture-in-picture top half or full-frame behind the hook line, his face back on screen within
4 s. If no clip shows the exact thing, use his own face, never a loosely related clip.

Sources, in order: the fighter's own account (ig-browse workflow, Business Discovery), @ufc / official promoters,
then fight-clip pages. Every clip goes through Fetch video (`reference`), slug `pro-<fighter>-<what>`.
