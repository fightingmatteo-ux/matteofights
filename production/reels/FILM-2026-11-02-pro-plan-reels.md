## Rules (credibility without lying)
- It's always **"the at-home version"** or **"inspired by"**, never "his exact workout". Only facts that are widely
  documented (listed per reel). No "endorsed", no "his coach told me".
- Matteo's face carries most of the screen time. Pro footage: 2 to 4 s clips, picture-in-picture or behind the
  first hook, never the whole reel (Instagram limits reach on mostly-reposted footage; your commentary is what
  makes it original). Clips come from the Fetch video workflow (IG works; YouTube needs YT_COOKIES).
- No metric units. Nothing on camera that loads Matteo's knee.

## L1. Lomachenko footwork
Fact used: as a kid his father took him out of boxing for about 4 years to do traditional Ukrainian dance.
- A (number): "Lomachenko spent 4 years doing dance instead of boxing."
- B (question): "Ever wonder why Lomachenko's footwork looks like that?"
- C (reframe): "Lomachenko's footwork isn't talent. It's years of drills nobody films."
- D (contrast): "Most people drill punches. Lomachenko drilled his feet."
- E (identity): "If your feet feel stuck when you shadowbox, this is for you."
- Cards: MON FEET: quick feet 4x30 s · in-out steps 3x1 min · pivots 3x1 min | TUE HANDS + EYES: tennis ball wall catch 3x1 min · 3 punches then angle out 4x2 min | WED ENGINE: skipping 5x2 min · burpees 3x10 | THU REACTION: slip or punch on a called cue 4x2 min · ball drop catch 3x1 min | FRI ROUNDS: 6x3 min shadowboxing, angle after every combo

## L2. Tyson peek-a-boo
Fact used: Cus D'Amato's peek-a-boo style, high guard and constant head movement.
- A (question): "Want head movement like prime Tyson?"
- B (lesson): "Steal this from Tyson: your head is never where they're aiming."
- C (contrast): "Most people block punches. Tyson made them miss."
- D (reframe): "Tyson's power started with his head movement, not his arms."
- E (curiosity): "There's a reason Tyson's opponents kept hitting air."
- Cards: MON SLIPS: slip line (string between 2 chairs) 4x2 min · bob and weave under it 3x1 min | TUE NECK + CORE: hand-resisted neck holds 3x20 s each way · sit-ups 4x20 | WED POWER: squat jumps 4x5 · explosive push-ups 4x5 | THU PEEK-A-BOO: high guard shadowbox, slip-slip-hook 5x2 min | FRI ENGINE: jump rope 6x3 min

## L3. Khabib pressure
Fact used: wrestling from early childhood under his father (the bear-wrestling video), known for relentless pressure.
- A (reframe): "Khabib's pressure isn't strength. It's cardio."
- B (question): "How does Khabib never get tired?"
- C (contrast): "Most fighters get tired chasing. Khabib makes you tired being chased."
- D (result): "Nobody could get up from under Khabib. This is the engine behind it."
- E (identity): "If you gas out every time you push the pace, start here."
- Cards: MON SPRAWLS: sprawls 5x10 · level changes 4x8 | TUE GRIP: towel or dead hangs 4x max · bag carries 4x1 min | WED ENGINE: 10 x (30 s burpees, 30 s rest) | THU DRIVE: wall drive 5x20 s · bear crawls 4x20 steps | FRI GRIND: 5 rounds, 1 min each: sprawls, push-ups, squats, mountain climbers, rest

## L4. Usyk balance
Fact used: his training videos are famous for unusual balance and coordination drills.
- A (curiosity): "Usyk's training videos look weird. That's the point."
- B (question): "Why does Usyk never look off balance?"
- C (contrast): "Big guys train strength. Usyk trained balance."
- D (lesson): "Steal this from Usyk: balance first, power second."
- E (reframe): "You're not slow. You're off balance."
- Cards: MON BALANCE: single-leg stand eyes closed 3x30 s/leg · single-leg shadowbox 3x1 min/leg | TUE COORDINATION: 2-ball juggling 3x2 min · cross crawls 3x1 min | WED FEET: lateral shuffles 4x30 s · switch-stance shadowbox 4x2 min | THU ENGINE: skipping 5x3 min | FRI ROUNDS: 6x3 min shadowboxing, angles both ways
