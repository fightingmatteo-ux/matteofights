**Next episode:** the most-requested fighter in the comments (Claude tallies them in the daily check). If they ask

## L1. Lomachenko (keyword LOMA)
Fact check: as a kid his father took him out of boxing for about 4 years of traditional Ukrainian dance (widely
reported). In 2016 to 2017 four opponents in a row quit on their stools (Walters, Sosa, Marriaga, Rigondeaux). He is
a southpaw.

**Open loop:** "It's not the dancing itself. It's what the dancing taught his feet."

**1. He never steps straight back.** "When you punch, he doesn't go backwards. He steps to the side. So after his
combo, he's standing where your punches can't reach him."
Clip: Loma landing, then stepping off at an angle.

**2. His feet never stop.** "Tiny steps, in and out, all the time. You can't plant your feet to punch someone who's
never in the same spot twice."
Clip: Loma's feet in range, small in-out steps.

**Re-hook:** "The third one is why people quit against him."

**3. The pivot.** "He lands, then pivots around his front foot. Now he's at your side and you have to turn to find
him. While you're turning, he's already punching again. Four guys in a row quit on their stools against him."
Clip: the pivot to the opponent's side, opponent turning.

## L2. Tyson peek-a-boo (keyword TYSON)
Fact check: style built by his coach Cus D'Amato. High guard, constant head movement, punches from close range and
angles. Tyson was small for a heavyweight (about 5'10"). His right hand to the body, right uppercut is his best-known
combination. His neck training is well documented.

**Open loop:** "It's called peek-a-boo, and it's three things."

**1. The guard.** "Gloves on his cheeks, elbows on his ribs. He turns himself into the smallest target in the ring."
Clip: Tyson's high guard walking forward.

**2. His head never stops.** "Side to side, all the time, even when nobody's punching. You can't aim at something
that's always moving."
Clip: Tyson bobbing and slipping as he closes distance.

**Re-hook:** "The third one is where his power came from."

**3. Every slip loads a punch.** "When he slips to his right, his weight goes onto his right leg. That right hand
is already loaded. Right to the body, right uppercut. His defence and his offence were the same move."
Clip: the slip into right hand body, right uppercut.

## L3. Khabib pressure (keyword KHABIB)
Fact check: wrestled from early childhood under his father Abdulmanap. Retired 29 and 0. Known for pinning
opponents on the cage and heavy top control.

**Open loop:** "Everyone says his wrestling. It's how he uses it."

**1. He walks you to the fence.** "His punches aren't there to knock you out. They're there to close the distance.
Every step forward puts you closer to the cage."
Clip: Khabib walking an opponent back to the fence.

**2. He never stops at one attempt.** "If the single leg doesn't work, it becomes a double. If that doesn't work,
it's a trip. You defend one, the next one's already coming."
Clip: a chain of takedown attempts on the cage.

**Re-hook:** "The third one is why nobody had answers for him."

**3. He makes you carry him.** "Once you're down, he puts all his weight on you and controls your legs. Every
second you're carrying him. You get tired. He doesn't. That's the pressure."
Clip: top control, opponent working to stand up.

---

## L4. Usyk balance (keyword USYK)
Fact check: southpaw. Undisputed at cruiserweight, then moved up and beat Joshua twice and Fury twice. His training
videos are known for unusual balance and coordination drills.

**Open loop:** "He was a cruiserweight beating giants. Here's how."

**1. His feet never stop.** "Small steps, side to side, the whole fight. Bigger guys never get a still target to
load up on."
Clip: Usyk circling with small steps.

**2. His head stays over his feet.** "Even when he's moving, he's never leaning. Head over his feet, every step.
That means he's ready to punch from any position."
Clip: Usyk moving, then punching without resetting.

**Re-hook:** "The third one is what all those weird drills are for."

**3. He punches on the move.** "Most people have to stop to punch. Usyk throws while he's moving sideways or
backwards, and lands. That's what all those strange balance drills are for."
Clip: Usyk landing while moving laterally or backing up.

## L5. Floyd shoulder roll (keyword FLOYD)
Already in FILM-2026-11-03-floyd.md. Fact check: retired 50 and 0. Shoulder roll (also called the Philly shell).

**Open loop:** "It's called the shoulder roll, and it's three things."

**1. Lead shoulder up.** "His chin is tucked behind his front shoulder. Your right hand hits his shoulder, not
his chin."

**2. Lead hand low.** "Across his stomach. It catches the jab and protects his body at the same time."

**Re-hook:** "The third one is the one nobody copies."

**3. The pull counter.** "When you throw, he leans back just out of range. You miss, and his right hand is already
coming back at you. That's why he barely got hit in fifty fights."
