## 2. Chess, but you get punched

Your words (founder.md): "People think combat sports is just violence. In reality it's about balance, technique and
flow." / "It's like chess, but higher pressure because you might get punched."

| V | Opening | Hook (verbatim) | On screen | Score |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A | contrast | "People think fighting is violence. Good fighters are playing chess." | violence vs **chess** | 8/10 |
| B | reframe | "Fighting isn't violence. It's chess, but the pieces hit back." | the pieces **hit back** | 8/10 |
| C | question (under-tested) | "Ever notice the best fighters look bored?" | why they look **bored** | 7/10 (Specific 1) |

Points (free talk, BUT / SO between them):
1. A feint is a question. You're asking "what do you do when I do this?" Then you remember the answer.
2. Round one is for reading, not for winning. (Our listicle "read round one" exists: say it your way, one line.)
3. The calm guy controls the fight. Panic is moving without a plan. SO the one who thinks, wins more than the one
   who hits harder.
4. Re-hook (~50%): "That's why the scariest guy in your gym is usually the quietest."
5. Pearl: "Anyone can throw hands. Fighting is knowing why you threw them."

Example: one real moment where you read someone and it worked (only a true one; skip if none).
Close: loop into the hook. Caption ask: "Send this to the friend who still thinks fighting is just violence."

## 3. The fighter test (3 questions)

| V | Opening | Hook (verbatim) | On screen | Score |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A | number | "Three questions tell you if you're a fighter or a hobbyist." | fighter or **hobbyist**? | 8/10 |
| B | identity | "Answer yes to all three and you don't train for fun." | 3 questions. **be honest** | 8/10 |
| C | contrast | "Some people train for fun. These three questions find out which one you are." | fun or **fight**? | 7/10 (Fast 1) |

Questions (one beat each, big red 1 / 2 / 3 cards, pause after each so they answer in their head):
1. "You get caught clean in sparring. Do you want it back, or do you want to know why it landed?"
2. "Driving home, do you replay the round?"
3. "If nobody ever saw you train again, would you still go tomorrow?"
Re-hook before 3: "The last one is the one most people lie about."
Close (on screen + caption): "Comment your score out of 3. Send it to your training partner and compare."
Note: the comment is the mechanic here (a self-score is a one-line answer), so it's allowed by creator-voice.md
("a question they can answer in one line from their own training").
