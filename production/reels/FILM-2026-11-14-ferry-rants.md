## RANT 1 (film now): the 600 baht trip
Only say what's true for your trip: the price, the route, what it included. If a point below isn't true for you,
skip it.

Hooks (pick the line, say it your way):
- A (contrast): "People spend thousands on a Thailand trip. I'm doing this one for 600 baht."
- B (question): "Want to know the best deal in Thailand? I'm sitting on it."
- C (result): "Koh Samui to Bangkok for 600 baht. Here's why this is my favourite way to travel."
- D (reframe): "You don't need to be rich to live well in Thailand. You need to know how it works."
- E (story): "I almost booked a flight back to Bangkok. Glad I didn't."

Body (talking points):
- What the 600 baht gets you (ferry, then the bus/train leg, whatever it was for you), versus a flight.
- What you get from the slow way: the view, time to think, time to plan the week.
- The fighter angle: the same mindset you use in training. Do the simple thing that works, skip the expensive
  version you don't need.

Ending: "Follow if you want the side of Thailand people don't post about."
