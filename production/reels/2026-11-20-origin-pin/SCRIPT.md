# Origin pin: "School came easy. Fighting didn't." (filmed 2026-11-20, at home)

The "who is he" pin (pin 2 in `FILM-2026-11-14-pinned.md`), replacing the 11-02 "where it started" talking
points. The research behind it: `production/competitors/talking-heads-2026-11-20.md`.

**Thesis (Matteo's own words, 11-20):** school came naturally, so he never had to work for anything. Training for
his first fight was the first time the outcome depended on the work. That's what fighting is to him: the thing
that taught him consistency, discipline, and how to actually get good at something. Now the knee is the same test.

**Rules for this reel**
- **Never mention a car, a crash, drinking or driving** (insurance, Matteo 11-20). Say "an accident". No wreck
  photos. Hospital, scan, brace, crutches and scar footage are fine.
- Never present him as a current fighter. "Comeback" means back to full training; no fight date, no surgery date
  (none planned yet).
- No first-fight result (KO/draw/ref stays out, decided 11-02). The fight footage stops before the clinch.
- No Stance, no business, no automation.
- Bridge lines are cut so each hook stands alone; the body starts the same way after every hook.

## The body (same after every hook, about 45 s)

Talk it, don't read it. Lines in **bold** are worth saying close to word for word; the rest is the gist.

1. **Where it started (fast, about 6 s):** "Karate at seven. Jiu-jitsu from ten to thirteen. Then a street fight
   showed me the mat isn't the street. So I switched to striking."
2. **School (the setup, about 6 s):** "Back then, school came easy. I never studied, and I still passed. **I'd never
   had to work for anything.**"
3. **The first fight (the core, about 10 s):** "Then I booked my first Muay Thai fight. I was sixteen, and I was
   scared. **Scared of getting knocked out in front of all my friends.** So if I hadn't trained that day, I
   couldn't sleep."
4. **The twist (re-hook, about 7 s):** "**The day before the fight, I got appendicitis.** Fight cancelled. Pushed
   back three months, at a different stadium. So I trained three more months."
   (In hook C this is the payoff: keep it, and the editor holds the board until here.)
5. **What fighting is to me (your words, about 7 s):** the gist: "That's what fighting is to me. **It's the first
   thing that taught me: if you want an outcome, you have to work for it.** Consistency. Discipline. How to
   actually get good at something."
6. **Since (fast, about 4 s):** "Since then, five countries. Thailand, Switzerland, Italy, Morocco, Brazil."
7. **What's kept fight two away (about 8 s):** "Then I moved to Bangkok. Boxer's fracture. And this March, the day
   before I went back to school, an accident in Koh Samui. Broken fibula. Torn ACL." (In hook D the accident is
   already the opening: the editor keeps only "Boxer's fracture" here.)
8. **Now (about 6 s, shadowbox while you say it or right after):** "**I can't jump. I can't spar. But I can do
   this.**" Then: "Same rule as that first fight. You want it, you work for it."
9. **Close (spoken + on screen):** "**Follow if you want to see the comeback.**" Option to film too: "**Follow if
   you want to see fight number two.**" (more specific; the pinned set uses "comeback", Matteo 11-14, so the editor
   cuts both and Matteo picks)

Loop take (optional, one continuous take): "...Follow if you want to see the comeback. Because school came easy
to me." The edit ends before the last sentence, so the replay runs into hook B.
