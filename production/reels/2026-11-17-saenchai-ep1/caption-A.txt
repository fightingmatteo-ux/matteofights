Bigger guys win the trade.
Saenchai never traded. He won Lumpinee titles in four weight classes, then spent years fighting heavier men.

3 things he does to bigger men:

1. He's never where the hit lands. One small step to the side, and the punch or the kick goes past him.
2. He catches the kick. Now the big man is standing on one leg, with no power.
3. The sweep. From the catch, he takes out the standing leg. The bigger guy goes down, gets up and has to start over.
