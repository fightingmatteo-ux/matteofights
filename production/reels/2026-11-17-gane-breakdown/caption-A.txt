Most heavyweights stand and trade.
Gane never does.

3 things that make a heavyweight move like a lightweight:

1. He never stands in front of you. A small step to the side after every exchange, so your best shots hit air.
2. His reach. A long jab and teep from the edge of range, and he's gone before you can answer.
3. He counters when you commit. Step in with something big and he's already moved, and the counter lands on your way out.
