You can spot who's dangerous in under two seconds. In any bar, in any meeting.

3 things fighters clock first:

1. Hands. Untrained guys talk with their arms wide. Fighters keep them close and loose.
2. Feet. One foot slightly back usually means he's done this before.
3. Eyes. He watches your hands or your neck, not your face.

Once you see it, you can't unsee it.
