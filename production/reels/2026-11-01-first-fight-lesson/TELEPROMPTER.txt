=== 1. BODY ===
(3 tries. Slate: "Body, one." / "Body, two." / "Body, three.")

[calm, low, like telling a friend at the bar]
I was sixteen. My first fight. His sixth.

[a little quieter, like you can still hear it]
Right before I walked out, someone told me: "If you don't knock him out, you lose."

[short, flat]
And I believed it.

[slower on "hunted"]
So I didn't fight him. I hunted him.

[matter of fact, like describing the tape]
We clinch. I throw elbows on top of his head.

[slow down, this is the moment]
The ref steps in to separate us.

[flat, no pride, slight shake of the head]
And I throw the right hand anyway.

[short pause before, then plain]
He goes down.

[look straight into the lens, slower]
They called it a draw.

[honest, half a second pause after]
And honestly? That was fair.

[firm, no smile]
That punch was late. When the ref steps in, you stop.

[quiet, two words, let it land]
I didn't.

[pick it back up, counting]
Three things I got wrong that night.

[one finger]
I let one sentence decide my game plan.

[two fingers]
I chased the knockout instead of winning rounds.

[three fingers, a bit slower]
And I kept swinging when I should've stopped.

[reflective, slightly smiling at your younger self, not at the KO]
I thought fighting was about hurting the other guy.

[firm, stronger, this is the line]
It's about control.

[steady, straight to the lens, hard stop at the end]
If you have to break the rules to get the knockout, you didn't earn it.

(STOP. Hold still 2 seconds. Don't say anything else.)
