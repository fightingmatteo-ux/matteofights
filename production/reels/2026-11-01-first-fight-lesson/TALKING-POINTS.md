# My first fight: how NOT to do it

**The angle in one line:** a 16-year-old chased a knockout so badly he hit a man after the ref stepped in.
The draw was fair. This is what that night taught him.

**Why this angle fixes the old one.** The knockout-draw cuts said "you be the judge" and left the late punch open
for debate. That reads as "I still think I won". Here Matteo gives the verdict himself, before any commenter can:
**the punch was late, the draw was fair, and here is what I learned.** Owning a foul out loud is the credibility.
Nobody can call you out for something you already called out.

## The three rules for this reel

1. **You give the verdict, not the comments.** Say "late" or "illegal" in your own mouth, early, calmly.
2. **The ref and the "you'll lose" line are not excuses.** They are the trap you fell into. Never say "robbed",
   "the ref knew him so...", or anything that sounds like the result was unfair to you.
3. **No pride on the KO.** Don't smile, don't say "he slept", don't replay the drop in slow motion. Real speed
   once, then a freeze on the punch with the ref's hands already between you. Calm face. You're older now.

## Talking points (film from these; say them your way)

1. **Setup.** 16. My first fight, his sixth. Someone told me before I walked out: "If you don't knock him out,
   you lose."
2. **The trap.** I believed it. So I didn't go in to fight him. I went in to hunt him.
3. **What happened.** Clinch. Elbows on top of his head. The ref steps in to separate us. And I throw the right
   hand anyway. He goes down.
4. **Re-hook / the verdict (slow down here).** "They called it a draw. And honestly? That was fair."
5. **Own it.** That punch was late. When the ref steps in, you stop. Every fighter knows that. I didn't stop.
6. **The three mistakes** (this is the "how not to" list):
   - I let one sentence decide my whole game plan.
   - I chased the knockout instead of winning rounds.
   - I kept swinging when the ref said stop.
7. **The lesson (the line people save).** I thought fighting was about hurting the other guy. It's about
   control. If you have to break the rules to get the knockout, you didn't earn it.
8. **Send line.** "Got someone with their first fight coming up? Send them this."
