# Talking points: You be the judge (my first fight)

Format: **"judge me"** (from the kaixerb reference: a reel that asks for a verdict turns viewers into judges, and
his got ~3% comments per like). Fighter version: the real footage of Matteo's first Muay Thai fight (age 16,
April 2023). He knocked the guy out with a right hand **while the ref was separating them**, and the official
result was **a draw** (context/founder.md). People will argue about the call and about his technique, and both
arguments happen in the comments. Edit in the editorial look (`reel-kit/editorial/`): white card on the verdict line.

Footage needed: the KO clip from the fight video (Drive). The talking head is the frame around it.

**Hook (verbatim, 3 versions for the A/B/C test):**
- A: "This was my first Muay Thai fight. I knocked him out, and the judges called it a draw. You be the judge."
- B: "I was sixteen. It was his sixth fight and my first. Watch the end and tell me if this was a draw."
- C: "Be honest. Rate my first fight out of ten. I was sixteen."

**Your points (free talk, short, over the fight clip):**
1. The setup: his 6th fight, your 1st, the ref knew him, someone told you "if you don't knock him out, you lose".
2. What happened: clinch, he's hanging on, elbow on top of the head, then the right hand as the ref separates you.
3. The honest part: "It wasn't clean at all." Then stop. Don't defend it; the comments do that.

**White card (the line that lands):** "Official result: **draw**." (keyword DRAW, slam + sub)

**Close + CTA (on screen + spoken, one line):** "Win, loss or draw? Tell me in the comments." Caption asks for a
rating out of ten and what you'd fix first.

**Why this should travel:** it's real, it's controversial on its own terms (a KO scored as a draw), and every
fighter has an opinion on a late punch. Judge it on comments per 1k views and follows per 1k vs the listicles
(LEDGER.md).
