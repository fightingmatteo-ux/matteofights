One. Lead shoulder up, chin tucked behind it. The right hand hits your shoulder, not your chin.
Two. Lead hand low, across your stomach. It catches the jab and covers your body.
Three is the one nobody copies. When they throw, he rolls, and the right hand is already coming back. That's the pull counter.
Here's how to train it at home. Monday to Friday.
Comment FLOYD and I'll send you the full week."
