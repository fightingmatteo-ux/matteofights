# Talking points: Why you freeze in sparring (3 things fighters do in their head)

Talking-head version of the two proven winners (both numbered listicles: 6 signs = 331k, 8 ways = 161k).
What carries over: a NUMBER promised up front (keeps them watching to the end), identity (viewer sorts
themselves: beginner vs fighter), and a usable payoff. Not a personal story. Each point turns on BUT or SO.

Film handheld at eye level, chin clear of the bottom edge. Say each point 2–3 times in your own words,
pause a beat between takes. Only the hooks are word-for-word. Target 30–35 s. Start talking at full
energy on the first word (no "hey guys", no breath in). Lean slightly into the lens on the hook.

**Hook (verbatim, 3 versions = 3 different angles for the A/B/C test):**
- A (pain): "If you freeze in sparring, it's not your technique."
- B (identity, the proven shape): "3 things fighters do in their head that beginners never do."
- C (contrast): "Beginners try to win every exchange. Fighters don't."

**Bridge (straight after any hook, verbatim-ish):** "It's 3 things in your head. Number 3 is the one
nobody does."

**Credibility beat (one line, only if true for you):** "Every good fighter I've trained with, in five
countries, does these."

**Your points (free talk, give each a number out loud):**
1. **They expect to get hit.** Beginners treat every punch they take like a mistake, so they panic.
   BUT getting hit is just information. Next spar: when you take a shot, say "fine" in your head and
   keep working.
2. **One job per round.** Beginners try to do everything, so they do nothing. Fighters pick one thing:
   "this round, only jab to the body." SO your head stays quiet and you actually learn something.
3. **They reset in one second.** (the one nobody does) Beginners carry the last bad exchange into the
   next one. Fighters step back, breathe out hard, and start again from zero. Next spar: after you get
   caught, one step back, one long exhale, reset.

**Close + CTA:** "Which one do you struggle with? Comment the number." + "Follow for the mental side of
fighting."

**Loop takes (one continuous take each, don't stop at the dash):**
- A: "…Follow for the mental side of fighting. — If you freeze in sparring, it's not your technique."
- B: "…Follow for the mental side of fighting. — 3 things fighters do in their head that beginners never do."
- C: "…Follow for the mental side of fighting. — Beginners try to win every exchange. Fighters don't."
