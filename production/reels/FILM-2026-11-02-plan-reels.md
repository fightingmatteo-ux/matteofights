## How to film (about 40 min for all 6)

- Phone selfie, eye level, face a window, room above your head (editorial look). Same spot and shirt for all 6.
- **Each hook is its own take.** Breathe, look into the lens, say it, hold still 1 s. Redo any you stumble on.
- Then say the middle line once ("Here's a 3-day plan. Day 1, Day 2, Day 3." counting on your fingers).
- Then the ending **twice**: once with PLAN, once with PROTOCOL. PLAN goes out once the plan branch is live;
  until then we use the PROTOCOL take so every comment still gets a DM.
- Name uploads like `P0-A`, `P0-B` ... `P0-body`, `P0-plan`, `P0-protocol` (Studio, upload footage).
- Optional b-roll: 5 to 10 s of shadowboxing or push-ups. **Nothing that loads the knee** (no jumps, no pivots on
  the bad leg). Drive clips cover the rest.
- Talking points, not a script: say it your way. Only say "I do this" if it's true.

The endings, same for every reel:
- PLAN take: "Comment PLAN and I'll send you the plan."
- PROTOCOL take: "Comment PROTOCOL and I'll send it to you free."
