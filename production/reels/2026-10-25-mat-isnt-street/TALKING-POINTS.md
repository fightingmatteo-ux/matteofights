# Talking points: The mat isn't the street

**Why this one, today.** Our talking heads hold attention (10 to 16 s avg watch, 2x the listicles) but stall at
0.6 to 1.3x views: the door is too narrow, only fighters stop (LEDGER, 10-22). This reel opens the widest door in
the niche, the "is BJJ the best self-defense?" argument that every gym, every comment section and every guy who
has never trained already has an opinion on. Then it narrows to the fighter-mindset lesson, which only Matteo
can tell because it happened to him (context/founder.md: three years of BJJ, a street fight, took the guy down
like a BJJ guy, tore a knee ligament on the pavement, "the mat isn't the street").

Built with the new script skills (`.claude/skills/studio-script`, `viral-hooks`, `viral-instagram-reels`,
`going-viral`):
- **Goal: SHARES** (going-viral). Breakouts get 1 share per 8 likes vs 1 per 55 on normal reels (Buzzfy playbook).
  The send is "you need this / this is so you": everyone knows a guy who says "I'd just take him down".
- **Structure: funnel** (studio-script `funnel.md`): bridge L (the BJJ self-defense debate) → tension (I did
  exactly what the mat taught) → pivot at ~30% (the gym has rules, the street has none) → pearl (the one line
  people repeat). Attacks an idea, never BJJ or BJJ people.
- **Proven nearby:** a self-defense talking head did 771k views / 69k shares (@helenschweitzer_com, Sept 2026);
  street-fight breakdowns 229k (@djhamp). Our contrast opening already won a hook test (freeze C).
- **Not an injury story.** The knee is one line of consequence. The reel is about the misconception.

**Target:** full cut 30 to 38 s (about 120 to 140 spoken words at your pace). Short cut D: 15 to 18 s.
**No spoken CTA.** The freeze reel lost people before the spoken ask. The send ask lives on screen and in the
caption. The video ends on the pearl and loops back into the hook.

## The body (free talk, say each beat 2 to 3 times, your own words)

Beats are joined by BUT / SO, never "and then" (viral-short-form retention rule).

1. **Setup (one line).** Three years of BJJ. I was good on the mat. Say it plain, no flex.
2. **The moment.** It went off in the street. I did exactly what the mat taught me: shot, took him down.
   Only say what actually happened. No extra details you're not sure about.
3. **The turn (pivot, ~30%).** BUT there was no mat. Concrete. My knee hit the ground and the ligament went.
4. **Re-hook (~50%, resets attention).** "The takedown worked. That was the problem."
5. **The reason (fair to BJJ).** BJJ didn't fail me. I brought gym rules to a place that doesn't have any.
   On the mat there's a ref, one opponent, a soft floor, a tap. Out there: none of it.
6. **The pearl (the line people repeat, say it slow, leave 0.5 s after).**
   "In the gym you're trying to win. In the street you're trying to get home."
   (Say it your way if that line isn't you. Keep the two-sides shape.)

**Loop line (one continuous take with beat 6, don't stop):**
- A: "...you're trying to get home. Learned that the day I took him down like a BJJ guy. The pavement took my knee."
- B: "...you're trying to get home. Because on the mat, BJJ wins. On concrete, it cost me my knee."
- C: "...you're trying to get home. Everyone says learn BJJ for self-defense. Nobody tells you about the floor."

**Short cut D (15 to 18 s):** Hook B → beat 4 "The takedown worked. That was the problem." → beat 6 pearl → loop B.
